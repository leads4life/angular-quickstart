道地 Authentic, not a template

The details other designers get wrong

Red and gold is easy. These are the checks we run on every site so it reads as yours, not as a theme.

金荷

Names checked for meaning

"Golden Lotus" translated literally is 金蓮, which is also an old term for bound feet. We used 金荷 instead. Every name gets that check.

梅花

Chinese motifs, not "Asian" ones

Plum blossom rather than cherry blossom, key-fret borders rather than Japanese wave patterns, real brush lettering instead of novelty fonts.

菜單

Real menu conventions

Numbered dishes, lunch specials with fried rice and an egg roll, pint and quart soups, Chinese names with pinyin.

滿月

Customs stated correctly

Red eggs and ginger for a baby's full month, uncut longevity noodles at birthdays, a whole fish at New Year. Small things your guests notice.

Questions

Good to know

Is Golden Lotus a real restaurant?
No. It's a fictional restaurant we created to show what we build. The menu, prices, address and photos are illustrative.
Can my site look different from the demo?
Yes. The demo is one design. Yours gets your colours, your name, your dishes and your photos.
Can you connect online ordering?
Yes. We link every order button to the ordering or delivery service you choose.
Do I need professional photos?
Real photos of your food and dining room work best. We give you a named shot list; if you don't have photos yet, we can plan illustrative images until you do.
Traditional or Simplified Chinese?
Whichever matches your sign and your customers. We keep the whole site in one system.
Can I change the menu later?
Yes. All your dishes and prices live in one place, so updates are quick.
