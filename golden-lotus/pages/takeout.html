Takeout Questions

Can you make a dish milder or spicier?
Yes. Ask for mild, medium or extra spicy when you order, and we adjust the chilies in the wok.
Do you cook without MSG?
Tell us when you order and we will leave out added MSG. Some sauces, like oyster and soy, naturally contain glutamates.
How do you handle allergies?
Let us know about any allergy when you order. Our kitchen uses peanuts, tree nuts, shellfish, soy, wheat, egg and sesame, so we cannot promise a dish is free of them.

Call %%PHONE%% with your address and we will tell you whether you are in our delivery area and what it costs.
