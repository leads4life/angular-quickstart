01Our goal

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We have not had the site formally audited.

02What the site does

  • A “Skip to content” link appears first when you press Tab.
  • Every interactive element shows a visible focus outline.
  • Menu items, prices and hours are real text that screen readers and translation tools can read.
  • Photos have text descriptions; decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Menu filters announce how many dishes are shown.
  • Forms label every field and move focus to the first field that needs fixing.
  • Animations are turned off if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Pages reflow to a single column on small screens without sideways scrolling.

03Known limits

Online ordering and delivery apps are run by other companies, and we cannot control their accessibility. If you have trouble, call and we will take your order by phone.

04In the restaurant

Service animals are welcome. If you would like to know about the entrance, seating or restrooms before you visit, call us and we will describe them.
