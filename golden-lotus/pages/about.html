Who We Are

A Neighborhood Kitchen With Deep Roots

Golden Lotus brings traditional Chinese cooking to Wellington: generous plates, bright sauces and the smoky edge only a very hot wok can give.

Our menu covers the Chinese-American dishes people grow up loving, from General Tso’s chicken to crab rangoon, next to the regional classics behind them: Shanghai soup dumplings, Cantonese chow fun and the numbing heat of Sichuan.

How We Cook

The “breath of the wok”: the smoky, seared flavor that only comes from a very hot wok and fast hands.

Cooked to order

Nothing sits under a heat lamp. Every dish is fired when you order it.

Fresh vegetables

Crisp broccoli, bok choy and snow peas, cut in our kitchen.

Made for sharing

Chinese meals are eaten family-style, so our portions and tables are built for passing plates.

Our Name

Why the Lotus

Our Chinese name is 金荷 (jīn hé): gold for good fortune, and 荷, the lotus of summer ponds.

Chinese scholars have loved the lotus for a thousand years because it “rises from the mud unstained” (出淤泥而不染), as Zhou Dunyi wrote in his Song-dynasty essay 《愛蓮說》, On Loving the Lotus. The two characters are carved into the red seal beside our name.
