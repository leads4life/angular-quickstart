Event details

Event Questions

Can we bring our own cake?
Yes. We will plate and serve it for you. Ask about any cake-cutting fee when you book.
Can we decorate the room?
Table decorations and balloons are welcome. Please no confetti or open flames other than birthday candles.
Can you handle dietary needs?
Yes. Tell us about vegetarian guests and allergies when you plan the menu, and we will adjust the courses.
How far ahead should we book?
Two to four weeks is ideal. Lunar New Year and December weekends fill up early.
