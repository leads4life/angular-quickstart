# VISUAL-DNA: Sugarline Bakehouse (SLB-2026-11)

**Brand look (owner-supplied):** warm & homemade.

## Palette by role
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Background, fresh cream | #FBF4E6 |
| Alt band, butter | #F6E3B4 |
| Accent, golden butter | #E9B949 |
| Primary ink and CTA fill, cocoa | #3B2618 |
| Secondary text, toasted brown | #7A5236 |
| Soft accent, strawberry cream (sparingly) | #E8A598 |

## Type
Soft, warm serif display (Fraunces / Recoleta feel); friendly humanist sans for body; an optional hand-script accent for short notes only.

## Motifs
1. Piped-buttercream scallop edge between sections.
2. Kraft cake-box tag tied with twine, used for labels, step numbers and price notes.
3. Recipe-card notes.

## Three distinct systems (for the mockup round)
- **A, "Kitchen Table":** full-bleed hero photo (S01) with a cream text panel overlaid on the empty left side. Large soft serif headline, generous calm spacing, alternating photo/text rows. Buttercream scallop seams between every section. Cream-dominant.
- **B, "Recipe Card":** split hero: a cream card on the left with the headline and recipe-card notes; the photo on the right sits inside a tall arched "window" frame. Rounded humanist type with a script accent, butter-yellow bands, kraft-tag numbered steps, tighter card-grid pacing. Seams are torn-paper/recipe-card edges.
- **C, "Cake Box":** a deep cocoa hero band with centred oversized serif headline in cream, a photo strip below with offset stacked photo frames like cake-box lids. High contrast cocoa/cream, golden-butter rules, kraft-paper texture bands, twine-tag dividers. Bolder, more editorial pacing.
