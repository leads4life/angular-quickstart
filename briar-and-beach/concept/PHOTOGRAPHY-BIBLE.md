# Photography bible: Briar & Beach Landscaping

One consistent set: real coastal New England (Massachusetts South Shore and Cape Cod) gardens and the people who build them.
- **Light:** late-afternoon gold, soft overcast or blue hour. Never harsh noon sun.
- **Grade:** warm highlights, gently muted greens, true sea blues, natural skin tones, slight film softness, no heavy HDR.
- **Materials:** weathered grey cedar shingles, white trim, granite and fieldstone, bluestone, teak, gravel.
- **Plants:** blue and white hydrangeas, beach roses, dune grass, sea lavender, ornamental grasses, boxwood, lavender.
- **People:** candid and mid-task, plain work clothes in sage, sand, navy or grey; no logos; nobody posing for the camera.
- **Never:** text, letters, numbers, signs, logos, brand names, house numbers, collages, borders, or real or famous people.
- **Text space:** each scene leaves one calm side for headlines (see photo-scenes.json).
