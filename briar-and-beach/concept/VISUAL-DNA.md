# Visual DNA: Briar & Beach Landscaping

**Palette:** Sea glass #7FA99B · Deep sea glass #3F7366 (buttons, links) · Sand #E8DCC5 · Shell white #FAF7F1 · Harbor ink #1E2B36 (text) · Driftwood #A3876A (accent)
**Type:** a soft, friendly serif for headings (Fraunces-like); a warm humanist sans for body text (Source Sans-like); sentence case
**Photo look:** natural coastal light, warm highlights, gently muted greens, true blues; documentary 35–50mm
**Motifs:** dry-stone wall edges · dune grass and shoreline seams · hand-drawn garden plans · weathered shingle texture
**Negative space:** generous; one idea per section

## Concept systems
- **A Shoreline:** full-bleed photo hero, headline over calm sky on the left; wavy shoreline seams and dune-grass sprigs; airy pacing, big photography; sea glass leads.
- **B Fieldstone:** split hero, with a sand panel on shingle texture beside a photo with a stone-wall edge; sturdy editorial grid, heavier serif, dry-stone dividers; harbor ink and driftwood lead.
- **C Garden Plan:** centred headline over a hand-drawn planting plan with a photo inset; staggered photos, fine ink linework, leaf sketches; shell white dominant; brisk rhythm.
