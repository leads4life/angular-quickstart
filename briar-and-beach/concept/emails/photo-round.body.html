Please create a set of 8 original photographs of the physical world of Briar & Beach Landscaping, a family-run landscaping company on the Massachusetts South Shore and Cape Cod. Two generations design and build coastal gardens, stone walls and bluestone patios for shingle-style seaside homes, install landscape lighting and irrigation, and look after the gardens season after season.

Look and light (every photo)

  • Photorealistic documentary style, like a professional editorial shoot for a home and garden magazine. Not illustrated, not 3D, not glossy stock.
  • Natural coastal New England light: late-afternoon gold, soft overcast or blue hour, never harsh noon sun. Warm highlights, gently muted greens, true sea blues, slight film softness, no heavy HDR.
  • Materials and plants: weathered grey cedar shingles, white trim, granite and fieldstone, bluestone, teak; blue and white hydrangeas, beach roses, dune grass, sea lavender, ornamental grasses, boxwood, lavender.
  • Palette in the scenes: sea glass (#7FA99B), deep sea glass (#3F7366), sand (#E8DCC5), shell white (#FAF7F1), harbor ink (#1E2B36), driftwood (#A3876A).
  • One consistent style and grade across all 8, as if shot by one photographer over one week on the same stretch of coast.

Rules (every photo)

  • No text, letters, numbers, logos, signs or brand names anywhere, including on trucks, clothing, tools, furniture, paper, house numbers and walls.
  • One standalone edge-to-edge photograph per file: no collage, contact sheet, border or frame.
  • Natural, candid people in plain work clothes (sage, sand, navy or grey), nobody posing for the camera; no real or famous people. Hands, tools, stonework and plants must look correct to a professional landscaper.
