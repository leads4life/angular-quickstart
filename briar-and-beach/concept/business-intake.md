## 2. Commercial model, offers and buyer journey

**How it makes money:** design-and-build projects bring in the most per job ($25k–$250k+, Fictional). Hardscape is usually part of those projects, sometimes sold on its own. Seasonal estate care contracts bring steady recurring income, and design clients often become care clients. Lighting and irrigation are add-ons and carry their own service visits.

**Offer hierarchy (Owner-supplied order of emphasis):**
1. **Landscape design & install** (lead offer): property walk, design plan, coastal-tolerant planting and installation.
2. **Hardscape & stonework:** bluestone patios, dry-stone and fieldstone walls, walkways, outdoor fireplaces and fire pits.
3. **Seasonal estate care:** spring and fall cleanups, pruning, lawn care and maintenance contracts.
4. **Outdoor lighting & irrigation:** landscape lighting, drip and irrigation systems, seasonal start-up and winterizing.

**Buyer journey:** the homeowner has a new, renovated or tired property → looks at a portfolio and decides whether the work fits their house → checks that a real local family stands behind the business → calls or sends a short quote form → free property walk → design proposal → build → moves on to seasonal care.

**Objections to answer:** "Will it survive salt, wind and sandy soil?" → coastal planting expertise. "Will they treat my property with care?" → a family name on the work and crews who stay with each project. "Will it look generic?" → design-led portfolio and stonework craft. "How long until it's done?" → a clear four-step process.

**Type (Concept proposal):** a soft, friendly serif for headings (Fraunces-like) and a warm humanist sans for body text (Source Sans-like). Sentence case throughout, no shouting.

**Motifs (Owner-supplied):** dry-stone wall edges, dune grass and shoreline, hand-drawn garden plans, weathered shingle texture. Each concept leads with one or two of these.

**Photography:** natural late-afternoon or soft overcast coastal light. Warm highlights, gently muted greens, true blues. A 35–50mm documentary feel, candid people and no posed stock smiles.

**A/B/C systems (Concept proposal):**
- **A "Shoreline":** a full-bleed photographic hero with the headline over calm sky on the left. Wavy shoreline section seams and dune-grass sprigs, airy pacing with large photos, and sea glass as the lead colour.
- **B "Fieldstone":** a split hero, with a sand text panel on shingle texture on the left and a photo on the right with a stone-wall edge. A sturdy editorial grid with heavier serif type, dry-stone dividers, and harbor ink with driftwood as the lead colours.
- **C "Garden Plan":** a centred headline over a hand-drawn planting plan, with a photo inset. Staggered photo layout, fine ink linework and leaf sketches, mostly shell white, quick rhythmic sections.
